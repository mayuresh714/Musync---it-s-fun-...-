Good to know

Plain words, not legal advice. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Privacy

  • Your songs stay on your device. When you add a song, your browser reads it and makes the light plan on your own computer or phone. The audio file is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • What is saved: your name, your email, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your light plans (small files with timings and colours, with no music inside). All of this is saved in this browser on this device.
  • Videos are made on your device and saved to your Downloads folder. We never receive them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address like any website that uses it.
  • You are in control. In My shows, Settings you can download your data or delete your account and every show on this device.
  • Because accounts live on your device, clearing your browser data removes them, and they do not follow you to another phone or computer yet. We cannot recover a lost password.

Terms

  • Musync is a free demo, provided as it is. It can change or stop at any time, and shows may look different between versions.
  • Only add songs and videos you own or have the right to use. You are responsible for what you add and for what you do with a video you make.
  • Do not use Musync to break the law or to harm others. Do not try to break or overload the site.
  • Light shows made by Musync are for visual simulation. Do not treat them as safety-checked plans for a real venue; always follow the rules and the advice of a licensed lighting professional for real events (for example, strobes can affect people with photosensitive epilepsy).

Copyright and music

  • We do not host, stream, sell or share commercial songs. The only audio on this site is demo music that Musync made itself, which is free to use.
  • Your own songs are never uploaded. The site keeps only the light plan (a WAL file). A light plan describes when lights change; it does not contain the recording, the lyrics or the melody.
  • To play a show with its song, you choose the audio file from your own device each time. It plays only for you, in your browser.
  • A video you save contains the song’s audio, so treat it like any other recording of that song: it is fine to keep it for yourself, but check the song’s rights before you post or share it publicly.
  • If you think something on this site infringes your rights, please open an issue on the project page on GitHub and we will look at it quickly.
